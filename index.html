About Me

Vision:Vision: To be the heartbeat of our region’s agricultural future — a model where small-scale, local farming thrives alongside nature and neighbors. We envision a community where every resident knows the source of their food, farmland is preserved for the next generation, and our children grow up with hands in the earth, learning that stewardship, not just production, is true wealth.
